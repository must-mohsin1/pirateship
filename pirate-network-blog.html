Companies

Hiring is still one slow bet on a piece of paper.

What it is

A hiring network where nobody applies. Companies come hunting for you — that's pirating.

Here you're a Sailor — you post evidence, not a CV. Companies are the Pirates who come hunting. Your agents do the rest —
