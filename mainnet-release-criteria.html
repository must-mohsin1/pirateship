What counts as release

The escrow owner may record release evidence only when every condition below is true:

  1. A public production Pirate Network product is available over HTTPS and is usable by supporters. A landing page, waitlist, mock-up, source repository, announcement, or Product Hunt preview is not enough.
  2. An approved supporter can prove control of the approving wallet and retrieve one stable, non-guessable PIRATE-POL-* product key.
  3. The released product accepts or validates that key and clearly explains the access it grants.
  4. The product, wallet-verification service, dedicated Polygon RPC, shared Redis datastore, backups, monitoring, and support process have passed the production canary.
  5. A durable public evidence URL identifies the usable product and its Product Hunt launch. It remains accessible after being recorded on-chain.

Usable product and release-evidence URL: Pending. The campaign will remain closed until it is published and verified.

Supporter terms

  • The first pledge from a wallet must be at least 300 POL. Later top-ups may be smaller while funding remains open.
  • The immutable deadline is exactly 30 days after contract deployment, measured by the Polygon block timestamp.
  • Approval is optional and permanent. It releases only that supporter's approved pledge to the beneficiary.
  • Supporters should approve only after reviewing and accepting the usable released product. The team cannot approve for a supporter.
  • If a supporter does not approve, the full funded pledge becomes refundable after the deadline.
  • A refund still requires an on-chain transaction. The supporter or another caller must submit it, and the contract sends the funds only to the original supporter wallet.
  • Native POL can change substantially in value. Polygon gas fees are separate from the pledge and are not returned by the escrow.

Approval and product keys

Product-key retrieval appears only after the supporter has approved on-chain and the service has verified a fresh wallet signature. Signing the verification message does not move funds. Approval itself is an on-chain transaction, cannot be undone, and makes that pledge withdrawable by the beneficiary.
